Hi, I'm Hyeonji Kim

I started out designing spaces for people and now use data to understand how they behave. In my free time, I enjoy shooting with a film camera to capture everyday moments and the spaces around me.

Background

I earned a B.S. in Landscape Architecture from Kyung Hee University. For my graduation project, I developed a master plan for a retirement community, designing spaces around how older adults move, rest and recover. This led me to a broader question: how do people interact with the spaces around them? That question drew me to data.

In Oracle Academy's Biohealth Convergence Data Analysis program, I built a prototype hospital infection-monitoring dashboard. According to an infection-control practitioner, it could cut patient-status review time from an hour to under 10 minutes.

Future Goal

I want to become a data analyst in digital health, identifying when users disengage and which notifications and features encourage continued use and physical activity.

In the long term, I aim to become a product data scientist focused on wearable health technology, designing experiments and developing personalized experiences that help users stay engaged and build healthier habits. Ultimately, I want to make healthy behaviors easier to sustain through products that fit into people's daily lives.
